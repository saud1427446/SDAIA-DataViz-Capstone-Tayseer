# Data Visualization & Storytelling

## Executive Summary (BLUF)
Recommendation to direct the SAR 40 million allocation specifically toward the three lagging regions (Najran, Northern Borders, and Al-Baha) to bridge the digital adoption gap and achieve the national 65% target within the 90-day execution roadmap.

## Project Scope & Deliverables
- **Decision:** Targeted SAR 40M regional investment.
- **Narrative Arc:** SCR Arc (Situation → Complication → Resolution) delivered via a 7-slide executive briefing.
- **Visual Evidence:** Visual analytics captured from the Tayseer synthetic services dataset.

## Visual Evidence Sources
1. **National Trend:** 48-month upward trajectory plateauing beneath the 65% benchmark.
2. **Regional Breakdown:** 8 lagging regions with critical shortfalls in Najran (60.9%), Northern Borders (62.7%), and Al-Baha (62.8%).
3. **Channel Economics:** Branch transactions cost 24.80 SAR vs. 15.66 SAR for Mobile App (+58% cost premium).
